How it works

Four steps from a piece of waste to a collected bin.

1. Capture

Choose a disposal location and upload a photo or take one with your webcam.

2. Classify

The CNN predicts plastic, paper, metal, glass or organic with a confidence percentage.

3. Route

The item is assigned to the matching compartment of a bin at that location and its simulated fill level rises.

4. Collect

Admins see almost-full bins, get alerts at 90%, collect, and reset capacity.
